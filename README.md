# EDS223 HW2: Exploring patterns of environmental justice

## Description

This repository contains files related to the HW2 assignment "Exploring patterns of environmental justice" for EDS223, part of the UCSB MEDS 2027 graduate program. In this assignment, we explored the following using data from EJScreen's 2023 environmental and socioeconomic metrics, the Digital Scholarship Lab at the University of Richmond's historical Home Owners' Loan Corporation (HOLC) data, and SWITRS 2020-2025 California pedestrian and bicycle crash data:

- Part 1: Legacy of HOLC redlining in current (2023) environmental (in)justice in Los Angeles (LA) county, CA.
- Part 2: Legacy of HOLC redlining in recent (2020-2025) pedestrian and bike accidents in LA county, CA.

## Data

The EJScreen data used for this assignment is from the EJScreen: Environmental Justice Screening and Mapping Tool, which no longer exists online. However, our 2023 data can be accessed through the following link: [https://drive.google.com/file/d/1nG6Nj1bXfzQFOVMO8Km3eNy4SWu1YcIQ/view](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1nG6Nj1bXfzQFOVMO8Km3eNy4SWu1YcIQ/view)

The HOLC data used for this assignment can be found at the following Mapping Inequality website: Nelson, R. K., Winling, L, et al. (2023). Mapping Inequality: Redlining in New Deal America. Digital Scholarship Lab. Retrieved October 21, 2026, from https://dsl.richmond.edu/panorama/redlining.[https://dsl.richmond.edu/panorama/redlining/data](https://dsl.richmond.edu/panorama/redlining/data)

The California Statewide Integrated Traffic Records System (SWITRS) pedestrian and bike crash data can be accessed through the Transportation Information Mapping System (TIMS) website: University of California, Berkeley Safe Transportation Research and Education Center. (n.d.). Transportation Injury Mapping System (TIMS). Retrieved October 21, 2026, from [https://tims.berkeley.edu/](https://tims.berkeley.edu/)
